
# Abstraction

- The idea of removing the details of the parts of a system to focus on a higher level

Ex: A Pizza Restaurant App 
    - you might ignore the details of individual orders such as the pizza type associated with it or the address to deliver the pizza to so that you can just model the higher level idea of an Order

Ex: Google Maps
    - Different zoom levels serve as different levels of abstraction. There are different details you're concerned with at different zoom levels
